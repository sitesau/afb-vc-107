An adelaide finance broker acts as an intermediary who sits between you and the lenders. This aligns the broker's incentives with your goal: securing a loan that settles successfully.

Understanding Costs and Lenders Mortgage Insurance

Costs are a major concern for anyone buying their first home. While the broker's service is typically free, you must still be aware of government fees and stamp duty. A significant expense for many first-time buyers is Lenders Mortgage Insurance, or LMI. This insurance protects the lender if you default on the loan and is generally required if your deposit is less than 20 per cent of the property value.

A good finance broker in Adelaide will clearly explain how LMI works and how it affects your repayments. They can help you calculate whether it is better to wait and save a larger deposit to avoid LMI or to proceed with a smaller deposit now. They can also check which lenders have more generous policies regarding LMI, potentially saving you thousands of dollars over the life of the loan.

The Application to Settlement Journey

The loan process follows a clear sequence that starts with discovery. The broker reviews your financial position to determine what you can afford. They then compare your profile against their lender panel to present suitable options. Once you choose a loan, the broker prepares and lodges the application on your behalf.

The lender then assesses the application, often ordering a property valuation. They may issue conditional approval before moving to unconditional approval. Finally, settlement occurs, and the funds are released to purchase your home. From the first call to settlement, a clean application typically takes four to six weeks. A broker keeps this timeline tight by anticipating what each lender requires and ensuring documents are correct the first time.

Common questions

Does a finance broker charge a fee for first home loans? For the vast majority of home loans, the broker is paid a commission by the lender, so the service costs you nothing. A licensed broker will disclose any fees or commissions in writing before you commit.

Can I get a loan if I have a small deposit? Yes, brokers know which lenders accept smaller deposits. If your deposit is under 20 percent, you will likely need to pay Lenders Mortgage Insurance, which the broker can factor into your budget.
